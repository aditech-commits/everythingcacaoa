Artisanal Heritage

Rooted in the Gold Coast

The story of how local tradition meets contemporary chocolate craftsmanship.

Pillar 01

The Soil & The Harvest

Ghanaian cocoa is renowned worldwide for its deep, rich cocoa baseline. We work hand-in-hand with generational farming families in Suhum, Assin Fosu, and Sefwi Wiawso.

Through fair-trade partnerships, we pay living income premiums well above international commodity rates, supporting sustainable farming practices and zero-child-labor commitments.

Pillar 02

The Artisan Touch

From micro-batch roasting in small drums to traditional winnowing, stone conching for 72 hours, and precise hand-tempering on marble slabs, our master chocolatiers oversee every detail.

We never introduce palm oil or soy lecithin. Only pure Ghanaian cocoa butter, unrefined cane sugar, and hand-selected natural ingredients.

Pillar 03

Sustainable Elegance

Luxury should leave a gentle footprint on the earth. Every bar wrapper is printed on 100% recyclable, biodegradable paper stock embossed with gold foil and vegetal inks.

Our zero-waste policy ensures cacao husks are repurposed into organic compost for local community gardens and tea infusions.
